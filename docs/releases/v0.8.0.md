## Overview

v0.8.0 delivers **Phase 8 Luxury Marketplace Experience** on top of the Phase 7 Social Commerce platform. Designer's Street now features serialized limited editions, stock scarcity signaling, rich editorial product storytelling, luxury badges & trust indicators, interactive campaign lookbooks, and enhanced designer house profiles.

Final QA verdict: **READY FOR FREEZE** (`docs/phase-8-final-qa.md`).

---

## New Features

### 1. Limited Edition & Serialized Scarcity
- Serialized edition labels (`#021 / 100`) and visual edition progress bars (`EditionBadge`).
- Real-time scarcity signals (`Only 2 Left`, `Selling Fast`, `Almost Sold Out`, `Recently Purchased`, `Limited Release`) via `ScarcityStrip`.
- Overselling protection & clean non-limited product fallbacks.

### 2. Luxury Product Storytelling
- Extended PDP tabs: **Care Instructions**, **Material & Craft Origin**, and **Designer Inspiration**.
- Shopping confidence grid (`TrustSignals`): Authenticity guarantee, premium packaging, secure checkout, easy returns, and handcrafted indicators.

### 3. Luxury Badges & Curation Signals
- Standardized badge resolution (`resolveLuxuryBadges`): `Verified Designer`, `Editor's Pick`, `Limited Edition`, `Made to Order`, `Handcrafted`, `Sustainable`, `Premium Collection`.

### 4. Interactive Lookbooks
- Lookbook cards grid on `/designer/[handle]`.
- Dedicated Lookbook detail view (`/designer/[handle]/lookbooks/[slug]`) with campaign hero and tagged product links.
- Full integration with **Universal Media Viewer (`MediaViewer`)** for continuous image and video slide playback.

### 5. Enhanced Designer Profiles
- Highlighting house design philosophy, studio location, years of experience, awards (FDCI, Elle Style), press mentions (Vogue India, Harper's Bazaar), and editorial gallery.
